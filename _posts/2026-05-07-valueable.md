### Book

“Letters to Live By: An Alphabet Book with Intention” Written by Lisa Frenkel Riddiough and Illustrated by Asa Gilland

### Response Art

After reading this book, I instruct the group to pick a value or intention that begins with the first letter of their name. Then, to write that value in a big font across their paper. Add colors, shapes, and pictures to reflect the value. If a participant would like to write intentions using all the letters in their name, they can!

### Process Questions

1. What intention or personal value is most important to you? How do the colors, shapes, or pictures you drew support this?
2. What gets in the way of you living out this value?

### Therapeutic Benefits

This book is simple yet intentionally written to help readers examine their own personal values and intentions for living. The playful and whimsical illustrations that reflect each value portrayed in the book can inspire group participants to think of their own through colors and illustrations. The art project is a strengths-based approach, deriving from positive psychology, and can increase self-awareness, promote higher self-esteem, and help foster actionable steps in line with one’s own personal values.

### Materials

- Paper
- Coloring pens
- Color pencils
