### Book

“Tilda Tries Again” written and illustrated by Tom Percival

### Response Art

After reading this book, I ask the group to create a topsy-turvy image. I include questions such as ‘what does topsy-turvy feel like?’ and ‘or does this feeling have a color or shape to it?

### Process Questions

1. What things or situations in life make you feel topsy-turvy?
2. What are some coping skills or strategies you’ve learned that help you when you’re feeling this way?

### Therapeutic Benefits

The book focuses on themes related to resiliency, acceptance, and developing coping skills when going through change or life transitions. Topsy-turvy changes could include a new diagnosis, being relocated to a new home, having your plans fall through, or even conflict in relationships. The art process following the reading assists in externalizing the topsy-turvy feeling as it becomes embodied through shape and color. The process increases self-awareness, building resilience and providing validation of felt emotions.

### Materials

- Construction paper
- Color pens and markers
- Color pencils
- Large tempera sticks
