### Book

This week, I used the book “Martin Rising” by Andrea Davis Pinkney & Brian Pinkney. We reflected on community, connection, and unity through art.

### Response Art

The process includes reading a select few poems from the book, discussing the themes, and tracing our hands or coloring paper in the shape of a hand to personalize with words or images. Participants were asked to add imagery or words that help them explore the content deeper, and to apply the content to their own lives if desired. Questions like ‘What change do you want to see in your own communities?’ ‘In yourself?’ and ‘How can we build a better future together?’ The hands were then all put together on a larger piece of butcher paper, with the fingers touching, to reflect community, unity, and inclusion.

### Process Questions

1. What was it like to trace your hand and add your own images or words representing community and change?
2. How was it intercrossing each other’s hands onto the larger piece of paper? What feelings came up for you?

### Therapeutic Benefits

The artwork evokes conversation around our own personal values and need for action. The overall process promotes a sense of agency and independence, increasing interpersonal skills and self-awareness.

### Materials

Skintone craft paper, coloring pencils/pens, gem stickers, gluestick, scissors, pencil
