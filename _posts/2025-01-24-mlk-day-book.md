### Book

This week, I used the book “Martin Rising” by Andrea Davis Pinkney & Brian Pinkney with seniors. We reflected on community, connection, and unity through art.

### Response Art

The process includes reading a select few poems from the book, discussing the themes, and tracing our hands or coloring paper in the shape of a hand to personalize with words or images. Participants were asked to add imagery or words that help them explore the content deeper, and to apply the content to their own lives if desired.

### Therapeutic Benefits

The artwork evokes conversation around our own personal values and need for action. The overall process promotes a sense of agency and independence, increasing interpersonal skills and self-awareness.

### Materials

Skintone craft paper, coloring pencils/pens, gem stickers, gluestick, scissors, pencil
