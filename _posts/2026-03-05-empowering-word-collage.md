### Book

The book we featured this week in my senior community was “Say Something!” by Peter H. Reynolds. It’s a great read focused around speaking up, using your voice and other nonverbal forms of self-expression. The book can increase self-esteem while promoting individuality and a sense of agency.

### Response Art

The art process following began with exploring a mixed variety of cut-out words (if you need ideas of words, pick some from the book!). Rearrange the words to make an encouraging phrase you want to tell the world or your community (if you don’t see the word you need, use alphabet foam stickers or write it!). If you prefer just picking one word, that works as well. Then, you glue the word or phrase to the center of the paper and make art around it. What additional images, patterns, or colors come to mind when you read this word or phrase?

### Process Questions

1. What word or phrase did you come up with? How does this word or phrase show up in your life or community?
2. Did you add any drawings or colors around the word or phrase?

### Therapeutic Benefits

The overall process of creating a word collage for yourself or community can shape personal values and build confidence. The book and the art fosters self-awareness and independence, increasing a sense of control for those who feel like their voice is lost, ignored, or forgotten.

### Materials

- 8.5” x 11” white printer paper, drawing paper or cardstock (feel free to go bigger or smaller)
- Pre-cut out words
- Glue
- Scissors
- Coloring pencils/pens, pencil and eraser
- Oil pastels
- Alphabet letter foam stickers
