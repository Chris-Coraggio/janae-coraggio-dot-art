### Book

_The Heart and the Bottle_ written and illustrated Oliver Jeffers

### Response Art

Mold air dry clay into the shape of a heart. Add tile pieces or other miscellaneous items to reflect how your heart has changed over the years. Paint your heart or add color if desired. If you don’t have clay, use paper and coloring utensils to draw a heart instead.

### Process Questions

1. What inspired the choice of colors and designs of your heart?
2. Does the color or design reflect your life experiences at all? If so, how?

### Therapeutic Benefits

The book helps provide language for navigating grief and loss. It is a gentle reminder that overprotecting our heart from enduring more hardship can stump curiosity, inner growth, and keep us from fully living as exemplified by the main character. The story validates all the complex feelings that come with grief and loss, and encourages supportive communities to come alongside those who’re grieving. The art portion gives participants the opportunity to share about their own life experiences and find acceptance in moving forward while still acknowledging what was lost.

### Materials

- DAS non-toxic air drying modeling clay (I like the white version)
- Tile or ceramic pieces
- Beads
- Paint if desired
- Coloring pens
- Paper
