I adore the concept of this coloring book. We use it in our homeschool. The prompts and verses are easy to talk through my with six year old while she colors. The paper quality and designs are good. I do wish that the coloring pages and prompts weren’t on facing pages. I like to tear them out and I have to keep up with the previous day’s coloring sheet so I can read the next days verse, but if you’re going to keep them in the book, it is great!
