#### What's the psychology behind it?

The workshop begins with a reading of a social-emotional children’s book, followed by artmaking for a more integrated and holistic approach to emotional regulation and increased cognitive functioning. By activating both the left brain (logic) and right brain (creativity) through reading and drawing, the process encourages mindfulness as both hemispheres work more in tandem. The artmaking experience has been proven to lower stress, assist with interpersonal communication skills, and boost overall self-esteem.

#### Who is the facilitator?

Me! I’m a board certified art therapist, artist, and author with clinical experience working in pediatrics, assisted living, and memory care. My [master’s thesis](https://scholar.dominican.edu/art-therapy-theses-dissertations/1/) uncovers the therapeutic value of children’s books and how responding through art-making can improve overall mental and emotional wellbeing.

#### What communities does Janae visit?

These workshops are inclusive to all ages and capabilities, fostering emotional literacy as well as creative self-expression in senior centers, churches and school communities, and nonprofit spaces.

#### What books do you read in the art groups?

I try to find social-emotional children’s books that are appropriate for educational use in classroom-sized settings. These themes usually derive from a strength-based and positive psychology approach that can elicit discussion around personal values, self-esteem, managing emotions, building self-awareness, and developing social skills. I will also include my own books such as Coloring God’s Love for Me for making art prayer journals.

#### Disclaimer

Although led by a board certified art therapist, these educationally-based art groups can feel therapeutic in nature, but are not therapy or art therapy. If you or someone you know are searching for a clinician to address therapeutic needs, please find a local clinician by visiting [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us) or by finding a registered art therapist in your area at the [American Art Therapy Association Art Therapist Locator](https://arttherapy.org/art-therapist-locator/). These workshops are only promoting overall wellbeing and health through fostering creative self-expression through art.
