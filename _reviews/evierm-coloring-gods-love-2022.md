What a GREAT IDEA. 100 devotionals geared for kids. A picture right next to the reading that really ties in to the story of the devotional. Literally as you read the page based on a verse in the Bible, the coloring illustrates the reading. God lights my path... you color stars, moon etc. It's a genius way to intertwine the senses so your child will remember the verse with coloring the description using listening, visual and tactile senses. I hope Janae Dueck comes out with more resources.
