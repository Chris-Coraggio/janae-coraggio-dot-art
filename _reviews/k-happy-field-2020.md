We got this as a gift. What a sweet book!!! My 2 and 3 yr old daycare kiddos are typically squirmy during story time, but they sat still and listened intently to the story. The pictures were captivating as well. It is such a great story teaching young kids that things in life might be hard and hurt at times, but those hard times can produce the most beautiful things in life if we let them. What a great book!!!
